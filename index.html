01 · Research interests

Natural conversation.
Responsive machines.

My current interests center on AI systems that can listen, think, and speak in real time—while making their speed, costs, and interaction quality measurable.

  • 01

    Full-Duplex Spoken Dialogue

    Exploring systems that listen and speak concurrently, with a focus on turn-taking, interruption handling, backchanneling, and speech–vision coordination.

    • Turn-taking
    • Omni-modal interaction
  • 02

    Efficient Inference & Speculative Decoding

    Using idle listening windows to prepare candidate outputs and verify them against incoming audio, reducing response latency while accounting for extra computation.

    • Speculative listening
    • Real-time inference
  • 03

    End-to-End Evaluation & Benchmarks

    Decomposing perception, decision, language generation, speech synthesis, and tool execution to build comparable timing and interaction metrics across architectures.

    • Latency analysis
    • Benchmark design
Current question

How can local model acceleration become a perceptibly faster interaction—without hiding the costs of speculation, rollback, and parallel execution?
